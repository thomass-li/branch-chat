Branch Chat

Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026 · Applies to the Branch Chat Chrome extension

Branch Chat lets you highlight text in an AI chat and open a side conversation about it. This policy explains what the extension reads, where that information is kept, and where it is sent.

At a glance

  • Branch Chat has no servers of its own. The developer never receives your chats, questions, images or keys.
  • Everything is stored in your own browser. It is sent only to the AI provider you choose, when you send a branch message.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no sale of data.

What the extension reads and stores

Branch Chat only reads a page when you act: when you right-click selected text and choose Branch chat, or press Update in a branch.

InformationWhy
The text you highlightIt is the subject of the branch.
The visible conversation on ChatGPT, Claude or GeminiSo a branch can remember the chat it came from. You can turn this off in Account → Answer preferences.
The questions you type and images you attach in branchesThey make up the branch conversation.
The address and title of the chat tabSo branches are saved with, and shown for, the right chat.
The name you enter during setupShown in the extension only.
Your AI provider API key, or the key created by Sign in with OpenRouterNeeded to send requests to that provider on your behalf.
Your settingsChosen provider, model and answer preferences.

All of this is stored locally with Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device. It is not synced to other devices and is deleted when you remove the extension.

What is sent, and to whom

When you send a message in a branch, Branch Chat sends a request directly from your browser to the one AI provider you selected. That request contains:

  • the copy of the original chat (unless you turned that off),
  • the highlighted text and the messages and images in that branch and the branches above it,
  • your API key, to authenticate with that provider.

The provider handles that data under its own privacy policy: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, OpenRouter. If you connect your own custom server, requests go only to the address you entered.

Sign in with OpenRouter opens OpenRouter's own sign-in page. After you approve, OpenRouter returns an API key, which is stored only in your browser.

What Branch Chat does not do

  • It does not collect your browsing history, and it does not read pages you have not chosen to branch from.
  • It does not log keystrokes, clicks or other activity.
  • It does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code.
  • It does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone other than the AI provider you choose.
  • It does not use your data for advertising, or to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • It does not run code downloaded from the internet. All code ships inside the extension.

The use of information received by Branch Chat adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Delete branches: hover a branch and press ×, or delete a whole chat from History.
  • Disconnect a provider: Account → Disconnect removes its key from your browser.
  • Sign out: removes your account name and all keys; branches remain until you delete them.
  • Stop sending the original chat: turn off "Remember the original chat" in Answer preferences.
  • Remove everything: uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored data. Use Export backup first if you want a copy.

Children

Branch Chat is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If what the extension does with your data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the contact details on Branch Chat's Chrome Web Store listing.
